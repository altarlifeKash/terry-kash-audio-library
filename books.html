New Testament Translation

The Kingdom Bible: New Testament

The first published New Testament translation developed from a consistently fulfilled (Preterist) perspective.

Translated from the Greek text, The Kingdom Bible: New Testament is designed to help readers interpret Scripture with Scripture. The overview of each New Testament book introduces its historical setting, explains symbols and idioms familiar to the Old Testament and first-century world, and provides a framework for understanding the New Testament within its original covenantal and cultural context.

This translation is not presented as a perfect or final work. It is offered as a faithful contribution to the continuing work of biblical translation—a foundation that future scholars and translators can examine, refine, improve, and build upon for generations to come.

Heaven's Hidden Revolution

Discover the unfolding story of God’s Kingdom and the transforming purpose of Christ’s finished work.

Heaven's Hidden Revolution invites readers to reconsider the biblical story through the lens of God’s Kingdom. It explores how the life and work of Christ reveal a revolution deeper than political or outward change—a transformation that begins within and reshapes how believers understand Scripture, identity, purpose, and daily life.
